# Contribution Guidelines

Thank you for helping students and families find useful college-planning resources.

## Suggesting a resource

Before opening a pull request:

- Search the list and existing issues to avoid duplicates.
- Make one pull request per resource or closely related group of changes.
- Link directly to the resource's canonical page or repository.
- Use the format `- [Name](URL) - Description.` and end the description with a period.
- Explain what the resource does, who it serves, and any important account, cost, region, or eligibility limitation.
- Prefer official, actively maintained, privacy-respecting, accessible, and free or meaningfully usable resources.
- Place the entry in the most relevant section and keep entries alphabetized by name.
- Check spelling, grammar, Markdown rendering, and the destination link.
- Use a specific pull-request title, such as `Add College Navigator` or `Fix FAFSA link`.

## What belongs here

Resources should directly help prospective college students or their families with at least one of these activities:

- discovering, researching, or comparing colleges;
- planning or completing applications;
- understanding admissions decisions and next steps;
- estimating costs or applying for financial aid;
- finding legitimate scholarships; or
- preparing for commonly used admissions tests.

Open-source projects should have a clear license, useful documentation, and evidence that they work for their stated purpose. A repository that is only a classroom exercise, a thin demo, abandoned, or trained on unexplained admissions data may be declined.

## Quality and safety

- Prefer primary and authoritative data sources over unexplained rankings or predictions.
- Describe commercial products neutrally and disclose meaningful paywalls.
- Do not submit referral, affiliate, or tracking links.
- Do not add essay-writing or application-automation tools that encourage misrepresentation or violate an institution's rules.
- Do not claim that an admissions-probability estimate is authoritative.
- Never include private student information, credentials, or application data.

## Updating an existing entry

Corrections, replacements for dead links, and clearer limitations are welcome. Include evidence for significant changes, such as a deprecation notice or the resource's current documentation.

By participating, you agree to follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
