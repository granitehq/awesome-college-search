# Awesome College Search [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

A curated collection of useful college-search, application-planning, affordability, scholarship, and test-preparation resources for students and families.

The list emphasizes trustworthy data, practical tools, and resources that are usable without a school-sponsored counseling platform. Availability, pricing, and admissions policies can change; verify important details with the college or program itself.

## College discovery and comparison

- [BigFuture College Search](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/college-search) - Search and compare colleges using filters for academics, location, campus life, affordability, and admissions.
- [College Navigator](https://nces.ed.gov/collegenavigator/) - Explore institutions with official data from the U.S. Department of Education's National Center for Education Statistics.
- [College Scorecard](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/) - Compare U.S. colleges using federal data on cost, completion, debt, earnings, fields of study, and admissions.
- [Niche College Search](https://www.niche.com/colleges/search/best-colleges/) - Browse college profiles, rankings, costs, admissions information, and student reviews.
- [Peterson's College Search](https://www.petersons.com/college-search.aspx) - Discover and filter colleges by location, major, school type, tuition, and other criteria.
- [Times Higher Education World University Rankings](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/world-university-rankings) - Compare universities internationally using overall and category-level ranking scores.
- [U.S. News Best Colleges](https://www.usnews.com/best-colleges) - Browse U.S. college rankings, profiles, majors, and admissions information; some content requires a subscription.

## Application planning and tracking

- [College Tools](https://github.com/granitehq/college-tools) - Open-source Google Sheets toolkit for researching colleges, comparing admission fit and costs, tracking applications and aid, and generating a shared deadline-aware task plan. ([Use the template](https://college-tools.granite-hq.com/getting-started).)
- [Common App](https://www.commonapp.org/) - Apply to participating colleges and manage shared application materials, requirements, and deadlines in one account.
- [Scoir](https://www.scoir.com/students/apply-with-scoir) - Free college discovery and application-management platform for students and families, including applications to participating Coalition colleges.
- [Student Ops](https://github.com/Apollo887/student-ops) - Open-source, AI-assisted college and career planning pipeline for evaluating schools, comparing aid, finding scholarships, and organizing application work. It includes essay-drafting workflows, so applicants should follow each institution's AI policies and remain responsible for their submissions.

## Costs and financial aid

- [CSS Profile](https://cssprofile.collegeboard.org/) - Submit financial information to participating colleges and scholarship programs for nonfederal institutional aid; application fees may apply.
- [FAFSA](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa) - Official application for U.S. federal grants, work-study funds, and loans; states and colleges also use it to award aid.
- [Federal Student Aid Estimator](https://studentaid.gov/aid-estimator/) - Estimate eligibility for federal student aid before completing the FAFSA.
- [Net Price Calculator Center](https://collegecost.ed.gov/net-price) - Find a college's calculator and estimate what students like you paid after grants and scholarships.

## Scholarships

- [BigFuture Scholarship Search](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/scholarship-search) - Search a large scholarship directory and save opportunities to a College Board account.
- [BigFuture Scholarships](https://bigfuture.collegeboard.org/pay-for-college/bigfuture-scholarships) - Earn entries for College Board scholarships by completing college-planning steps; eligibility rules and deadlines apply.
- [CareerOneStop Scholarship Finder](https://www.careeronestop.org/toolkit/training/find-scholarships.aspx) - Search scholarship, fellowship, grant, and award listings sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor.

Never pay to submit a scholarship application or share sensitive financial information without verifying the sponsor. The [Federal Trade Commission's scholarship-scam guidance](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-avoid-scholarship-and-financial-aid-scams) explains common warning signs.

## Test preparation

- [ACT Free Test Prep](https://www.act.org/content/act/en/products-and-services/the-act/test-preparation/free-act-test-prep.html) - Official practice tests, quizzes, study guides, and test-taking strategies from ACT.
- [Bluebook](https://bluebook.collegeboard.org/students) - Official College Board app for full-length digital SAT practice tests and test-day previews.
- [Khan Academy Official Digital SAT Prep](https://www.khanacademy.org/test-prep/digital-sat) - Free lessons, examples, and practice exercises covering Digital SAT Math and Reading and Writing.

## Data and reference

- [College Scorecard Data](https://collegescorecard.ed.gov/data/) - Download federal higher-education data, read its documentation, or access it through an API.
- [College Scorecard MCP Server](https://github.com/cyanheads/college-scorecard-mcp-server) - Open-source MCP server for searching, comparing, and analyzing College Scorecard data on costs, earnings, programs, and outcomes.
- [collegedata.fyi](https://github.com/bolewood/collegedata-fyi) - Open-source Common Data Set archive and API that preserves institution-published documents and joins them with IPEDS and College Scorecard data.
- [Common Data Set Initiative](https://commondataset.org/) - Definitions and reporting standards behind the institution-published Common Data Set used by many college-search resources.
- [IPEDS](https://nces.ed.gov/ipeds/use-the-data) - Official U.S. postsecondary institution data with downloadable files and analysis tools.
